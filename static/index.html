    How to read North & South charts

    South Indian chart keeps zodiac signs fixed clockwise; H1 marks your Lagna.

    North Indian diamond chart keeps House 1 fixed at the top center diamond and counts counter-clockwise; numbers 1–12 show the Rashi (1=Aries … 12=Pisces).

    Click any house or planet row to see why it matters and where its ruler sits.

    An empty house is completely normal and never means a bad outcome.
